Chapter 21

Gravity field and Boxes

  1. Push the right Box over the Switch and up into the corner.
  2. Keep it there until the gravity field switches off.
  3. Run left. Jump over the Switch as you push the other Box.
  4. Climb up the ledge.
  5. Wait for the Box to fall back down.
  6. Push it underneath the ladder at the top of the hill.

Swap the Boxes (optional, for Secret Egg)

  1. This part is optional. You need it to get a Secret Egg.
  2. Go back down the hill. Push the bottom Box left.
  3. Jump up the ledge. Return to the top Box.
  4. Push the top Box down the hill and over the other Box. The Boxes have now switched places.
  5. Use the box on the right to get the left Box up the hill.
  6. Push the left Box under the ladder.

Secret Egg

Gun and Secret Egg #7

  1. Make sure the bumpy side of the Box faces up.
  2. Push the Box left toward the remaining gun above.
  3. Stand on the Box to trigger the gun. Run as it gets shot.
  4. Repeat this a couple of times.
  5. Push the Box right. Take Secret Egg #7 from inside.

Sinking ladder and gravity field

  1. Return one of the Boxes under the ladder before you climb up.
  2. Head right. Climb down a different sinking ladder.
  3. Push this Box right into the corner like the last one.
  4. Run left. Climb up the ladder.
  5. Rush left. Pull the first Box onto this ledge while it is up high.
  6. Do not let the Box hit your head on the way down from the ceiling. It can kill you.
  7. Push the Box so it is just left of the ledge above.
  8. Activate the gravity field below, just like before.
  9. This time, stand on the Box after you climb up the ladder. You rise with the Box and the gate.
  10. Jump right through the open gate.
Achievement

Elevator and chain

  1. Walk right. Make sure the elevator is up.
  2. Hang from the ledge under the elevator.
  3. Jump right to grab the chain hanging from the middle of the elevator.
  4. Climb down the chain until you reach the floor. If you fall from it, you die.
  5. Walk to the glowing spot to get the Under Ground Achievement.

Elevator and machine gun

  1. Climb back up the chain. It is hard to spot, but it is there.
  2. Use the panel to lower the elevator.
  3. Use the panel again to ride the elevator up.
  4. Hit the next panel to lower the elevator once more.
  5. Use the panel again. Jump on top of the elevator.
  6. Run right as you touch the beam of the machine gun. This helps release the Box.
  7. Lower the elevator. Push the Box to the left edge of it.
  8. Use the panel and ride the elevator up. Climb on top of the Box.
  9. Jump left to the ledge above the machine gun. Go left until you see a panel.

Catching the falling Box

  1. Hit the panel to turn off the gravity field. A Box falls.
  2. Let the Box slide right a bit. Then start to raise it with the field.
  3. Do not let it hit the ceiling or the ground just above you.
  4. The Box keeps its momentum from sliding down the hill and lands at your feet.
  5. This takes a few tries and some juggling.

Gravity Switch and moving ledge

  1. Push this Box as far right as it can go. Jump the gap to meet up with the first Box.
  2. Drop down and lower the elevator.
  3. Push this Box all the way right to block the beam of the next machine gun.
  4. Climb to the top of the nearby ladder. Speed past more machine gun shots.
  5. Hit the Gravity Switch. You end up on the ceiling.
  6. Walk left to find the other Box that you released earlier.
  7. Push both Boxes right so one lands on the right end of the moving ledge below.
  8. Move the other Box to the left of the Gravity Switch.

Stack the Boxes (optional, for Secret Egg #8)

  1. This part is optional. It needs a lot of patience and a few precise moves. You can replay the chapter later for it.
  2. Wait for the ledge to begin moving left.
  3. Hit the Gravity Switch to drop down.
  4. Pull the lower Box so your back is right up against the moving ledge.
  5. Keep doing this until the second Box falls and stacks on top.
  6. Hold the bottom Box as the ledge pushes you all left.

Stack on the ceiling

  1. Push both Boxes left slowly. The slower you move, the less the top Box slides.
  2. A sudden, brief pull in either direction on a flat surface lines up the top Box. It slides the opposite way.
  3. Push the lined-up stack left of the Gravity Switch, on flat ground.
  4. Walk to the right side of the Gravity Switch. Stay as far from the stack as you can.
  5. Hit the Gravity Switch. The Boxes stay stacked as you all hit the ceiling.

Secret Egg

Secret Egg #8

  1. Push the stack all the way left to the wall. Start slow, then go to medium speed. This keeps the Boxes from sliding and you from getting stuck.
  2. Make any corrections on flat ground.
  3. At the wall, place the top Box a bit more left than right, but not enough to fall over.
  4. The Boxes now form steps. Climb up.
  5. Jump left from the top Box to the ledge.
  6. Walk left and pick up Secret Egg #8.

Moving ledge and next chapter

  1. Move both Boxes back right to the area with the moving ledge.
  2. Push both Boxes right so one lands on the right end of the moving ledge below.
  3. Move the other Box to the right of the Gravity Switch, above the bigger rock on the ground.
  4. Wait for the ledge to begin moving left. Hit the Gravity Switch.
  5. Quickly push the closer Box right into the moving ledge. Climb onto it.
  6. Push the second Box right to reach the next ledge.
  7. Climb down the ladder. Walk right to reach the next chapter.
