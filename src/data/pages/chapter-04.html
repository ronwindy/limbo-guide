Chapter 04

Hill and boulder

  1. Slide down the next hill carefully.
  2. Jump quickly before you land in another pit.
  3. Push the boulder right until it rolls on its own.
  4. Jump back left before it squashes you.

Rolling boulder

  1. Go right across the new path. Wait on a large boulder.
  2. Move left on top of it as it starts to roll.
  3. The spider chases you. Stay on the boulder until you are close to the next ledge.
  4. Jump right to the ledge.
  5. Tumble down the hill to lose your sticky cocoon.

Cavern and rope

  1. Run right to a decoy. You crash into a cavern below.
  2. Jump to the next rope. Jump off quickly as a Trap falls at your head.
  3. Jump back to the rope.
  4. Jump to the next ledge once the Trap has fallen past you.

Last hill

  1. Run down the next hill.
  2. Jump across a few gaps to reach the next chapter.
