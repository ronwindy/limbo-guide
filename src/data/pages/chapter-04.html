Chapter 04

Carefully slide down the next hill and jump quickly, before you land in another pit. Push the boulder right until it rolls on its own, then jump back left before you get squashed flat!

Go right across the new path and wait on a large boulder, then move left on top as it starts to roll. Even though your old friend the spider is trying to chase down his escaped meal, stay on the boulder until you are really close to the next ledge. Jump right to the ledge and tumble down the hill to finally lose your sticky cocoon.

Run right to a decoy as you crash into a cavern below, then jump to the next rope and quickly jump off as the Trap tries to cave your head in. Jump back to the rope and jump to the next ledge once the Trap has fallen past you to the ground.

Run down the next hill and jump across a few gaps to reach the next chapter.
