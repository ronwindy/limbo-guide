Welcome to the Limbo wiki guide.

LIMBO tells the timeless tale of a boy trying to find his lost sister, only with more decapitations, brain-eating slugs, gigantic spiders and hair-trigger traps than you can shake a severed limb at. With our help we'll make sure that you can survive all of these and more, and conquer any dream. Yes, even that one where you're writing a final exam in your underwear and everyone is talking backwards.
