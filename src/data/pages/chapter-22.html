Chapter 22

Blocks and ceiling

  1. Turn on the Magnet Switch to magnetize the ceiling.
  2. Turn on the Gravity Switch until the first block touches the ceiling.
  3. Turn off the Gravity Switch before the second block reaches the ceiling. This sends it back to the ground.
  4. Jump to the left ledge. Then jump on top of the right block.

Cart and rope

  1. Go right. Use the next Gravity Switch to end up on the ceiling.
  2. Listen for a cart with a rope travelling up to you.
  3. Hit the Switch when the cart is about half a cart length from the right edge of the screen.
  4. Drop down. Do a running jump to the rope.

Roof and last switch

  1. Run right. Watch the ground.
  2. Drop off to the roof by the smoke, just before you get impaled.
  3. Slide down the roof.
  4. Hit the sideways Gravity Switch in the air to reach the next chapter.
