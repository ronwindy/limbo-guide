Chapter 22

Turn the Magnet Switch on to magnetize the ceiling, then turn on the Gravity Switch until the first block touches the ceiling. Quickly turn off the Gravity Switch before the second block reaches the ceiling to send it back to the ground.

Now you should be able to leap to the left ledge and on top of the right block. Go right and use the next Gravity Switch to end up on the ceiling, then listen for a cart with a rope travelling up to you. Hit the Switch when the cart has about half a cart length between the right end of the cart and the right edge of the screen, then drop down and do a running jump to the rope.

Watch the ground as you speed right and drop off to the roof by the smoke, just before you get impaled. Slide down the roof and hit the sideways Gravity Switch in the air to reach the next chapter.
