Chapter 12

Letter H and O

  1. Watch the timing of the letter H lighting up. Lit areas kill you.
  2. Jump so you land on the left side as the light switches off.
  3. Quickly jump to the right side and the letter O. This also turns off the letter T.

Power Switch

  1. Drop down. Swing across the rope to clear the gap.
  2. Jump to the Power Switch. This shuts off the rest of the sign.
  3. Swing back left. Climb the pipe on the wall.

Secret egg (first half)

  1. For another secret egg, you can do half of the task now.
  2. The letter O spins when you weigh down its upper corners or walk slowly on them.
  3. Turn the O so the small bump on its outer edge faces right.
  4. Then continue to Chapter 23, or use the menu if you have been there before.

HOTEL sign and cart

  1. Jump across the HOTEL sign. Watch for the turning of the letter L.
  2. Leap to the arrow. Quickly jump to the next ledge before the arrow falls.
  3. Keep right across a glass dome.
  4. Pull a small cart away from the wall.
  5. Get on the right side of the cart. Push it until it starts to roll down the hill.
  6. Quickly climb to the top of the ladder.
  7. Wait for the cart to get close to the dome.

Glass dome and rope

  1. Jump off into the glass. You crash through and slide down a hill.
  2. Quickly jump across a pair of hanging lights to a rope.
  3. Ride the rope down.
  4. Drop safely to the ground. Continue to the next chapter below.
