Chapter 09

Secret Egg

For Secret Egg #2, go back left to the Box. Push it left until it hangs about 1/3 over the water.

Secret Egg #2

  1. Go back right. Follow the steps below until it starts to rain.
  2. Return to the Box.
  3. The rain knocked the Secret Egg loose. It sits on top of the Box.

Creature and wheel

  1. Walk right past a creature. Climb the ledge on your right.
  2. Jump left to the platform with glowing things under it.
  3. Jump on the right edge of the platform. Glowing food shakes loose.
  4. Drop down on the right side of the creature as it comes out of hiding.

Wheel and log

  1. Chase the creature left into the wheel. This makes it spin.
  2. Push the Lever left until it starts raining.
  3. Climb the right ledge. Keep going until you drop into a small hole.
  4. Grab the log on the right side. Pull it left to about the middle of the hole.

Water pipe

  1. Jump left. Climb the rope against the wall to get out.
  2. Look left between the two legs of the water pipe.
  3. Hang off the rope to connect the pipe above.
  4. Wait for the hole to fill with water.
  5. Quickly jump right across the floating log.

Moss and hill

  1. Leap to the right side of the water. Keep right until liquid pours ahead.
  2. Climb the dangling moss instead.
  3. Go right down the slippery hill.
  4. Push the Box right at the bottom to reach the next chapter.
