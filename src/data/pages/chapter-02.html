Chapter 02

Achievement

Climb the tree to get the Altitude is attitude Achievement.

Box and vine

  1. Go right until you reach flies near a Box and some water.
  2. Grab the Box. Push it left.
  3. Climb the Box to the vine by the second tree on your left.

Branches and achievement

  1. Swing from the bottom of the vine. Jump to the branch on your right.
  2. Jump left and right to work up the branches.
  3. Find a vine among the leaves up high. Jump to it from the right branch.
  4. Swing left from the vine to get the Altitude is attitude Achievement.

Broken tree piece

  1. Swing back right. Jump to the branch again.
  2. Push the broken tree piece off into the water.
  3. Climb back down the branches the way you came up.
  4. Go right to see where the piece landed.
  5. Push the piece right into the water. Climb on top to cross.

Trap and exit

  1. Go right. A Trap sits on a branch above you.
  2. Keep going right to reach the next chapter.
