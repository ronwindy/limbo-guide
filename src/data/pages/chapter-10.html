Chapter 10

Gap and flood

  1. Look up for a gap in the ledge above.
  2. Leave the Box at the left side of the gap.
  3. Climb the ladder. Pull the object right to open the gap fully.
  4. Drop down. Pull the nearby Lever left to flood the room.
  5. Climb the ladder quickly.
  6. Jump left to your floating Box as it rises through the gap.

Second Box

  1. Jump to the next ladder. Climb up quickly.
  2. Rush right. Pull a second Box left so it floats in the open room.
  3. Ride the Box until the water reaches the top of it.
  4. Jump up to the next ledge. Slide down the next hill a little.

The ball

  1. Jump to the second ledge so you do not drown below.
  2. Run past the ladder. Pull back the cover to let the ball reach you from below.
  3. Quickly push the cover back right as the ball rises and lands on top.
  4. Push the ball right down the hill.

Rising Box and Levers

  1. Run back left. Jump up the ladder.
  2. Jump right at the top. Wait on top of the Box.
  3. The ball rises under the Box and floats you to the next ledge.
  4. Go right past the light. Pull the bottom Lever left to balance the water above.
  5. Push the Lever right to drop the block a little.
  6. Climb the ladder. Jump across to the second Lever.

Blocks

  1. Pull the second Lever left to drop another block.
  2. Jump right across the blocks to the other side of the water.
  3. Keep right to the next chapter.
