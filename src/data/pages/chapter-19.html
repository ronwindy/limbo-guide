Chapter 19

Gears

  1. Push the Lever left. This reverses a few gears.
  2. Watch the right gear. Wait until the top corner of a tooth is level with your ground.
  3. Push the Lever right. The gear rises a bit more and pauses for a second.
  4. Jump right on top of the tooth.
  5. Jump back left to the next ladder before the right gear spins down again.

Ladders and carts

  1. Climb the ladder. Jump left to ride a tooth up to the next ledge.
  2. Go right to a small cart with a ladder on it. Ignore this ladder-cart.
  3. Jump to the next ledge.
  4. Pull the bigger cart so its right side is under the Lever above you.
  5. Go back left. Push the ladder-cart to the right side.
  6. Climb the ladder. Jump off at the left edge on the first pass of the cart.
  7. Wait by the Switch. Press it when the cart comes back left on the third pass and is about halfway to you.
  8. Quickly jump to the top of the ladder. On the fourth pass, leap to the high ledge when the ladder is close enough.

Power Switch and saw

  1. Run to the right end of the ledge.
  2. Jump to the Power Switch. Pull it as you fall onto the cart below.
  3. This electrifies the tracks and sends you rolling right to a ladder.
  4. Climb up. Keep the cart a bit ahead of you, then run right.
  5. Jump over the saw that is destroying your ledge.
  6. Land back on the cart below.

Cart and Button

  1. Wait until the cart nears another ledge. Drop off the left side of the cart before you touch the ledge.
  2. The cart hits a Button. This electrifies the ledge above.
  3. Push the cart left, away from the Button. Stop when you can just jump from it to the right ledge.
  4. Climb onto the cart. Quickly jump to the ledge.
  5. Run right. The cart rolls toward the Button again.
  6. Leap into the air before the cart touches the Button. Land clear of the electrified track.

Spinning room

  1. Push the Lever right as the room starts to spin and the light starts swinging.
  2. Walk right past the boulder and over a small bump.
  3. Wait when you see a ladder on the wall to your right.
  4. Jump across the gap to the ladder when you hear the boulder start rolling.
Achievement

Cross the wall of sparks to get the Guided By Sparks Achievement.

Box and sparks

  1. Jump the next gap as the room stops spinning. A Box falls to your right.
  2. Push the Box right to the wall of sparks. Climb on top.
  3. Jump left to a hidden platform in the air.
  4. Do a running jump right from this platform over the sparks.

Electrified floor

  1. Push the Box left over the edge as it slides along an electrified floor.
  2. Climb down the left end of your ledge. Drop onto the Box below.
  3. Walk right as the area gets brighter to reach the next chapter.
