Chapter 03

Spider legs

  1. Go right. A giant spider shows itself.
  2. Watch for the one sharp leg that tracks you.
  3. Wait in one spot until the leg rises. Then run before it spears you.
  4. Do this once more. You hear a Trap fall.
  5. Go left to find the Trap.

Trap the leg

  1. Jump left over the Trap. Push it right toward the spider.
  2. Wait for the spider to raise its leg again.
  3. Move the Trap to the spot where the leg will hit.
  4. Run left at once. The leg makes the Trap snap shut on it.

Other legs and hammock

  1. Do the same for 2 more legs. Watch for a revenge stab after the first of them.
  2. Go right into the tunnel. Follow it until the spider gets his revenge.
  3. Wiggle back and forth to break free of the hammock.
  4. Jump right. Hold the jump button for longer jumps.
  5. Leap over the hole in the ground to reach the next chapter.
