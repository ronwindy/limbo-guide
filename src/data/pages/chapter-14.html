Chapter 14

First elevator and Box

  1. Go past the elevators to a Box.
  2. Pull the Box left to the elevator.
  3. Hit the Switch to make the elevator rise. Ride it up with the Box.
  4. Push the Box right beside the top Switch. Leave it near the left edge.

Achievement

Urban Exploration

  1. Hit the Switch to lower the elevator. Hit it again to make it rise.
  2. Quickly jump from the Box onto the elevator. Wait near the left side.
  3. Jump left to a rope before you get crushed against the ceiling. This releases the glowing Urban Exploration Achievement.

Seesaw and Lever

  1. Jump right to the Switch. Jump back left to the ground.
  2. Go left and pick up the achievement.
  3. Take the elevator back up.
  4. Push the Box onto the left side of the nearby seesaw.
  5. Make the elevator go down. Grab the right side of its bottom.
  6. Drag it left onto the seesaw as the top of the elevator descends.
  7. The seesaw is now weighed down. Push the Box up towards the new Lever.
  8. Go right and pull the Lever to lower a rope.
  9. Push the Box close to the rope. Push the Lever right.
  10. Jump from the Box to the rope. Ride it up.
  11. Swing to the next ledge on your right.

Two Boxes and saw

  1. Hit the first Switch to start releasing the first Box.
  2. Press the second Button after the first Box starts to fall.
  3. Quickly pull the first Box right under the second one. It must drop on top.
  4. Climb up both Boxes. Do not push the top Box left.
  5. Quickly jump over the saw to the ledge above.

Achievement

Dark tunnel

  1. Walk right past a ladder.
  2. Jump right at the water in the dark tunnel.
  3. Get the Alone in the Dark Achievement on the ledge.
Secret Egg

Secret Egg #3

  1. There are three more Secret Eggs down here.
  2. Walk right to the wall. It is really a box.
  3. Pull the box left enough to climb on top of it.
  4. Jump right from the box to another ledge.
  5. Continue right for Secret Egg #3.

Secret Egg

Secret Egg #4

  1. Keep right to a real wall. Jump to climb over it.
  2. Work your way down, going back and forth.
  3. Fall down the larger shaft. You break through the ground at the bottom.
  4. Go right to find Secret Egg #4 under a beam of light.
Secret Egg

Secret Egg #5

  1. Walk right to what looks like another wall.
  2. Climb down the ledge you are standing on. Go right under the wall.
  3. Go right to the next wall. Climb over it.
  4. Walk right across a metal surface. A Secret Egg is below.
  5. Drop down on the right side of the metal surface. Face left.
  6. Pull an object to your right. Jump over it to get Secret Egg #5 inside.

Next chapter
