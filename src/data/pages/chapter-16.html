Chapter 16

Door and platforms

  1. Go right until you hit a Pressure Switch. This opens a door.
  2. Jump right. Go under the platform with the door.
  3. Climb the first ladder. Pull the Box left a bit.
  4. Climb the second ladder above. Head left.
  5. Pull the first platform right to jump over the handle. Then pull it all the way left.
  6. Pull the next platform right. Jump over and hit the panel to release a wheel below.
  7. Quickly jump right. Push the platform left to meet the wheel.
  8. Pull it all the way right to meet the next platform.
  9. Push that platform right to carry the wheel down a shaft.

Wheel and gear Lever

  1. Climb back down both ladders. Push the Box left to the door.
  2. Go left at the bottom to meet the wheel.
  3. Pull it right to the Pressure Switch.
  4. Jump right through the open door.
  5. Quickly pull the Box left to jam the door.
  6. Push the Box left through the door.
  7. Push it right along the bottom. Jump to the Lever by the large gear.
Achievement

Climbing the Cog

  1. Ignore the Lever for now.
  2. Take a running jump from the right edge of this platform. Climb onto the nearby tooth of the gear.
  3. Continue over the gear. Fall down the right side to get the Climbing the Cog Achievement.

Gear and Boxes

  1. Climb the ladder. Go back left over the gear.
  2. Pull the Lever to start the gear moving.
  3. Push your Box right onto the gear. Ride the gear across.
  4. Keep pushing the Box right past the gear and past the Pressure Switch. Stop at the next wall.
  5. Go left and climb the ladder to find a second Box. Push it left onto the gear.
  6. Follow the Box down. Push it right onto the Pressure Switch.
  7. Jump right from the Box to the ledge that rises from the Switch.
  8. Use the Box you placed here earlier to reach the ledge above.

Rope and electrified floor

  1. Ignore the panel. Swing on the rope to your right until you cannot swing higher.
  2. Jump left. Quickly use the panel to start the rope moving right.
  3. Run and jump to the rope when it swings close to your side.
  4. Swing across the electrified floor section.
  5. Drop down to a small gear and the next chapter below.
