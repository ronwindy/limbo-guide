Chapter 07

Ladder and Boxes

  1. Slide down the hill. Climb a ladder.
  2. Jump left to the first Box. Jump over to the second one.
  3. Jump straight up to break the rope holding this Box.
  4. Push the Box right down the hill and against the wall.
  5. Jump up the next ledge. Walk right to some water.

Water and bodies

  1. Jump on the body floating in the water.
  2. Wait for the next guy to dive in. Jump off his back to cross.
  3. Drag the last body with you.
  4. Push him right into the next pit. This disables a big Trap.
  5. Keep right. Go down a steep hill.
  6. Walk right when you see a big Trap above you.

Crushing Traps

  1. Jump onto the small rectangle under the Trap. This avoids it.
  2. Jump again to the other side.
  3. Do not step on the lower spots beside the middle. They crush you.
  4. The next Trap works the opposite way. Do NOT touch its middle section.

Enemies and spikes

  1. Jump over the Trap. Leap the spikes.
  2. Wait on the left side of the body. The enemies' darts fall short.
  3. Jump back over the spikes as the enemies chase you.
  4. Jump over the middle section of the first Trap again.

Last Traps

  1. Jump over the sides of the next Trap. It crushes the 2 remaining enemies.
  2. Go back right to the spikes. Jump them.
  3. Head right to a ladder and the next chapter.
