Chapter 11

First brain slug

  1. Jump the small gap.
  2. Carefully pull the Box left as the brain slug swings right.
  3. Go right. Let the brain slug take control.
  4. Jump over the Box to the ledge above until the slug reaches the light.
  5. Walk back right. Jump over the Box.
  6. Climb the first ladder.
  7. Jump to the second ladder when water is not falling between them.

Broken pipe

  1. Climb down the second ladder.
  2. Jump across the water as a water pipe breaks.
  3. Keep jumping right until the slug turns you back left.
  4. Jump back to the ladders, then over the Box to the high beam of light.
  5. Go back right over the Box and up the ladders.
  6. Wait at the top of the right ladder.

Rising water

  1. Wait for a pipe to surface as the water rises.
  2. Jump to it when the water covers the spikes.
  3. Leap to the next ledge. The creatures above swallow your brain slug.
  4. Pull the broken pipe piece out of the water.
  5. Push it as far right as it goes.
  6. Jump left from the pipe to reach a rope.

Achievement

Achievement and gap

  1. Drop from the rope.
  2. Pick up the It's Stuck Achievement on the pipe.
  3. Climb the ladder. Go right to a wide gap.
  4. Take a careful running jump across the gap.
  5. Slide down the next hill. Jump the gap at the bottom.
  6. Leap right to a pipe above some water.
  7. Climb to the top of the pipe for the next ledge.
  8. Keep right to the next chapter.
