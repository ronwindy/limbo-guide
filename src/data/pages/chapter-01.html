Chapter 01

Achievement

Walk left after you stand up and touch the glowing egg to get the Wrong Way Achievement. Then go right.

Log, box and rope

  1. Go right. Walk up the fallen log.
  2. Slide down the hill. Jump over the spikes.
  3. Stand next to the Box on the rope. Hold the Action Button and pull the Box left until the rope is tight.
  4. Jump onto the Box.
  5. Climb down the rope on your right. Do not drop down.

Water and boat

  1. Jump left onto the ledge at the bottom of the rope.
  2. Jump right over the water.
  3. Go right through the tunnel.
  4. Jump onto the boat. Ride it across the water.
  5. Jump off on the other side.
  6. Pull the boat right to the wall.
  7. Climb onto the boat. Jump right to the next ledge.

Tree and forest

  1. Climb the tree. Jump left to the rope.
  2. Climb the rope to the next ledge.
  3. Go right through the forest. Watch the ground for small rows of spikes.

Traps and flies

  1. The spikes are Traps. Hold the Action Button to drag each one, then jump over it.
  2. Keep going right until you see flies over a carcass.
  3. Walk under the flies. Drag the Trap on your right under them.
  4. Go left to the ledge. Jump above the flies to set off the Trap. This makes your rope lighter.

Log and boulder

  1. Go right down the hill. Jump onto the log.
  2. Quickly jump back left over the ledge when the log shifts and a boulder rolls at you.
  3. Climb to the top of the log. Go right to start the next chapter.
