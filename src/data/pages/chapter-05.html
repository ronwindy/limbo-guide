Chapter 05

Broken tree

  1. Look at the broken tree with spikes in it.
  2. Jump on the branch that props it up.
  3. Jump left quickly as the branch breaks.
  4. Go right after the spikes miss you.
  5. Ride the ledge as it falls right.
  6. Jump to a narrow platform.

Swinging ball and spider

  1. Jump straight up as a ball swings down from the right. It crashes through a few ledges.
  2. Slide down the next hill.
  3. Jump over the spikes at the bottom.
  4. Keep right to find a suspicious spider.
  5. Run under the leg when it rises. You see the puppeteer on the other side.

Water, tire and ladders

  1. Jump over the water on your right.
  2. Run back left. Jump over the water when you hear a rolling tire.
  3. Jump back right. Jump the smaller debris quickly.
  4. Go under the fire to the first ladder. Climb it.
  5. Jump left at the top to reach one more ladder. Climb it.
  6. Go right at the top to enter the next chapter. You see a hanging body.
