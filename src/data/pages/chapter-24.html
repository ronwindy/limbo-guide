Chapter 24

First gun and block

  1. Listen to the beeps. They speed up for the timed gravity switching.
  2. Leave about 7 beeps. Jump right and deactivate the Magnet Switch.
  3. Jump into the air. You get pulled to the ceiling as the gun fires.
  4. Run right as you and the block fall back down.
  5. Jump onto the block before you get shot into pieces.

Ledge and next gun beam

  1. Run across the block. Wait on the right end of the ledge.
  2. Wait until gravity is close to sending you up (about 7 beeps).
  3. Drop down to the next gun beam.
  4. Quickly jump left into the air. Land on the underside of your former ledge.
  5. Run left to the gap, fast. When the block falls, you slide safely down the hill.

Big saw

  1. Do not stand under the big saw. It slices you when gravity shifts again.
  2. Do a running jump right when gravity is close to changing from normal to inverted (about 9-10 beeps).
  3. Do not land on the bottom saw. You still fly up past the top saw after the change.

Final saw and barrier

  1. Wait for a fresh timer on the ceiling. Wait about 7 beeps before walking right.
  2. Line up with the right Gravity Switch. Do not hit it yet.
  3. Wait for gravity to send you falling back down.
  4. Hit the right Gravity Switch to dodge the final saw.
  5. Go crashing through the barrier.
  6. Walk right a bit farther to finish the game.

Try for the No Point in Dying Achievement. Play the whole game again in one sitting and die 5 times or less.

You can also hunt for the Secret Eggs to raise your completion above 100%.
