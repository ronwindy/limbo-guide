Chapter 23

Saw and letter H

  1. Run up away from the saw. Wait for the sign to light up.
  2. Jump off and hit the downwards Gravity Switch.
  3. Run right quickly while the letter H sign is off.
  4. Jump to the safe section in the middle.

Pipe above the saw

  1. Wait for the saw to start moving down again.
  2. Jump left from the letter H to land on top of the pipe.
  3. Stand on the right edge of the pipe.
  4. Count to 6 or so once the saw leaves the bottom of the screen.

Secret Egg

Secret Egg #10 (optional)

  1. Do a big running jump left. Hit the Gravity Switch on your way down.
  2. You now fly right. Make sure to land above the saw. If you land below it, restart.
  3. Play like normal. Use the next Gravity Switch to jump back to the safe spot on the letter H.
  4. Jump to the next ledge on your right.
  5. Secret Egg #10 falls out of the pipe above.

If you skipped the Secret Egg

  1. Run and jump right to a ledge when the sign turns off.

Rope to the letter O

  1. Wait for the sign to light up.
  2. Jump left from this ledge so you land as the sign is off.
  3. Quickly jump back right to grab a rope.
  4. Climb down to the letter O at the bottom.

Secret Egg

Secret Egg #9 (optional)

  1. You need to have spun the small letter O in Chapter 12. Then a handle shows on the right side of the large letter O.
  2. Pull the handle.
  3. Collect Secret Egg #9 from inside.

Timed Gravity Switch

  1. Walk right. Jump to activate the timed Gravity Switch.
  2. Jump back left.
  3. Drop to a passage in the ceiling.

Achievement

Box and left wall

  1. Quickly pull the left wall to release the Going Up Achievement.
  2. Pick it up. Drop back down.
  3. Activate the timed Gravity Switch about three more times.
  4. Each time, push the Box farther right. Wait on the left ledge before the field turns off.
  5. Make sure the Box falls down long enough.
  6. Watch out: the Box can crush your head as it rises each time.

Saw and final ledge

  1. When the Box is close enough to the right wall, use it to climb onto the ledge.
  2. Walk past the saw.
  3. Wait here until you fall and start to slide down the hill.
  4. Jump carefully. Use the next Gravity Switch.
  5. Run right to the ledge and the final chapter.
