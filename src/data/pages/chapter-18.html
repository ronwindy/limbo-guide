Chapter 18

Spinning room and gear

  1. Push the Lever right to start the room spinning again.
  2. Wait for the Box to drop to your level.
  3. Push it right to the ledge. Climb on top.
  4. Quickly jump to the next ledge.
  5. Keep running so the flap above does not squash you.
  6. Go right across a large gear. Another brain slug attaches to you.

Brain slug and conveyor

  1. Climb back left over the gear.
  2. Carefully jump from the gear tooth to the ledge.
  3. Run left until the slug moves you back right.
  4. Jump over the gear again. Jump to the conveyor belt just ahead.
  5. At normal walking speed you move slowly left on the conveyor. Use this and your run to get close to the Trap.
  6. Jump into the Trap as it rises. Quickly leap the next gap.

Traps and Boxes

  1. Approach the next Trap. Jump to its left side and hang off it. Do not climb up yet.
  2. Wait for the Trap to hit the ceiling. Then hold up to climb as soon as you can.
  3. Run and jump the next gap.
  4. Jump over the Boxes in your way.
  5. Leap from the top of one of the Boxes to remove the brain slug.
  6. Push and pull a Box so you can climb on top of it.
  7. Hang off the right side of the Trap to your left.
  8. Sneak through as it starts to sink. Jump to the ladder on your left.

Achievement

Backtracking

  1. Climb to the top. Head right.
  2. Pick up the Backtracking Achievement between the teeth of the gears.
  3. Reach the next chapter.
