Most Achievements / Trophies in Limbo are tied to collecting glowing "eggs." The remaining Achievements are based on completing the game.

Wrong Way (5)

Head left at the very start to find the egg.

Altitude is Attitude (5)

In Chapter 2, push over the broken tree. To its left, a nearly invisible rope hides behind the foreground leaves. Jump to it and climb up to the egg.

It’s Stuck (10)

At the end of Chapter 11, before the tall ladder (after you lose your brain slug), push the pipe on the ground right, past the ladder, into the water under the pipe that is NOT leaking. Then jump for the rope hidden behind a foreground beam. The egg drops onto the pipe.

Urban Exploration (10)

In Chapter 14, ride the large elevator to the second level, get off, and send it back down. Call it back up with the button, then jump left onto its roof. Quickly jump to the high rope on the left and climb to the egg.

Alone in the Dark (10)

Hard. At the end of Chapter 14, past the spinning saw blade and the two crates, a dark cave leads away from a ladder. Going deep into it kills you, but an invisible pit can be jumped. Time your run carefully, leap the chasm and take the egg.

Climbing the Cog (10)

In Chapter 16, don't flip the switch in front of the giant gear. Jump onto the gear and ride it right to the egg.

Backtracking (10)

Just after losing the brain slug in Chapter 18, you land on a conveyor belt. Push a box right, then climb on it to reach the edge of the crusher on the left. Hang there until the crusher recedes, climb up, cross it and take the ladder in the upper left. The egg is up here.

Guided by Sparks (10)

After the level stops rotating in Chapter 19, don't push the crate off the ledge. Keep it near the electrified wall and use it to climb to a hidden ledge on the left. Make a large leap right to the egg.

Under Ground (10)

At the elevator in Chapter 21, go to the ledge on the right. Tap DOWN to hang, then jump right to grab a chain. Climb down to the egg.

Going Up (10)

Activate the first gravity switch in Chapter 23, just past the "HO". Move the crate right and stand over the switch. When gravity reverses, hit the switch again as you fall, then go left to a hidden area. Pull the lever for the final egg.

Where Credit is Due (100)

Complete the game.

No Point in Dying (10)

Finish the game without quitting or dying more than five times.
