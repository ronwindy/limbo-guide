Chapter 08

Secret Egg

For Secret Egg #1, do the steps in "Secret Egg #1" below. It gives no achievement. It adds 1% above the normal 100%.

Secret Egg #1

  1. Hang off the rope above the gears. Climb up as it sinks.
  2. Jump left. Climb the ladder to the top.
  3. Run quickly left. The small pit of spikes has no spikes now.
  4. Drop in and grab the Secret Egg.
  5. Jump out of the pit before the spikes return.

Cart and first rope

  1. Go right. Climb down the ladder.
  2. Pull the cart out of the left alcove.
  3. Push the cart right as far as it goes.
  4. Climb the ladder. Jump right to the rope above the gears.
  5. Keep climbing the rope as it sinks. A gate rises off-screen.

Second gate

  1. Wait until the rope stops sinking. The first gate is now as high as it goes.
  2. Jump left. Run left off the ledge.
  3. Run right along the bottom to the cart you pushed.
  4. Climb on top of the cart. Jump to the bottom of the rope before it is pulled up.

Brain slug and the Box

  1. Swing right. Jump to the next rope.
  2. Weigh the rope down until the second gate is high enough. The first gate must not be low enough to block you yet.
  3. Jump off. Run through both gates.
  4. Go down the hill. Keep right until a glowing brain slug falls on your head.
  5. The slug controls your walking. Let it walk you left, then right down a hill.
  6. Jump the gap at the bottom. Jump over the Box until you walk left again.
  7. Push the Box left this time.
  8. Climb on the Box when it is just right of the three creatures above.
  9. Jump up so they eat the brain slug.
  10. Keep right to the Lever to reach the next chapter.
