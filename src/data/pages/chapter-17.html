Chapter 17

Gears

  1. Push the small gear right so it connects with the larger one. This starts the whole area moving.
  2. Stand near the left edge by the electrified floor as the ground flattens. This keeps you from being squashed by the wall.

Secret Egg

Secret Egg #6

  1. Run right to the next big gear. Ride the teeth up.
  2. Hold right as you enter the shadowy passage in the wall.
  3. Keep moving right. Jump as needed to find Secret Egg #6.

Magnet Switch and Box

  1. Go back left once the gear has stopped moving.
  2. Jump to the next ledge on your left.
  3. Ignore the panel. Drop down to a Box and a Magnet Switch.
  4. Hit the Magnet Switch to deactivate it.
  5. Push the Box right to the wall.
  6. Run left and activate the magnetic field.

Spinning room

  1. Jump right from the Box to the first panel. Use it.
  2. Run left. Jump from the Box to the ladder before the door closes.
  3. Go left. Pull the Box under the chain.
  4. Climb the chain. Hang on as the room spins.
  5. Drop back onto the Box when it stops under you. This avoids the electrified floor.

Saw and boulder

  1. Ride the Box left.
  2. Jump over the saw to the next rope before the saw destroys the Box.
  3. Drop down the hill.
  4. Jump over a boulder to reach the next chapter.
