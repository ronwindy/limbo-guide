Chapter 06

Swinging Traps

  1. Walk right carefully until you are near two wooden boards in the background.
  2. A Trap swings right from your left. Stop here and it misses you.
  3. Rush right a bit after it swings past. A second Trap swings left from your right.

Rope, ledge and log

  1. Climb the dangling rope left by the second Trap.
  2. Jump two gaps up here as the next rope is pulled up.
  3. Drop off the right side of the ledge. Do not land in the Traps on the left.
  4. Go right to a small log. The spider surfaces.
  5. Push the log into the water. Climb on top.

Logs and see-saw

  1. Jump to a small ledge in the water. Jump to the log beyond it.
  2. Run right to a log balanced like a see-saw.
  3. Wait on its right side until the spider puts his foot on the other end.
  4. Jump to the next ledge while you are in the air.

Boulder and spider

  1. Push the next log over. Jump the gap beyond it.
  2. Run right to a boulder propped up by a tiny twig.
  3. Jump on the twig to release the boulder.
  4. Run left toward the spider. Hug the right wall.
  5. The boulder rolls over you and into the spider.

Spider's end

  1. Jump the gap. Cross a larger log.
  2. Slide down the hill beyond it.
  3. Go right to some spikes. Turn around to see the spider's remains.
  4. Dodge one last strike from his only remaining leg.
  5. Grab the end of the leg. Rip it off.
  6. Push the spider's body right into the spikes. Jump to the other side.
  7. Walk past a bird and some hanging Boxes to reach the next chapter.
