Chapter 13

Start and brain slug

  1. Go left and touch the standing post.
  2. Run right a bit to avoid the falling debris.
  3. Go right. A brain slug will attach to you.
  4. Walk left until the slug points you the right way.
  5. Walk right.

Gaps and saw

  1. Keep right past the elevators.
  2. Do a running jump across the wider gap.
  3. Jump two more gaps.
  4. Step on the Button. This starts a deadly saw and slowly drops a Box.
  5. Hold left to walk slower. This gives the Box time to fall in front of you.
  6. Climb onto the Box. Take a running jump over the saw.
  7. Do not wait too long. The saw will split the Box, then you.

Brain slug and platforms

  1. Climb the next ledge quickly.
  2. Jump up to remove the brain slug.
  3. Jump back left over the saw as it nears the wall.
  4. Jump left to the next platform.
  5. Wait on its right edge until it dips and starts to rise.
  6. Run and jump to the left edge. Wait until it dips and starts to rise.
  7. Repeat this 6-7 times to get the platform moving more.
  8. When the right side dips, jump to the left edge. Jump to the next platform.

Last platforms

  1. Pull the left platform of the pair as far right as you can. Let it go.
  2. Jump on quickly. Run to the left edge.
  3. Jump to the next ledge.
  4. Go left for the next chapter.
