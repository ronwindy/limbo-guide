Chapter 15

Fly ride

  1. If you missed Alone in the Dark or any of the 3 Secret Eggs near it, climb back down the ladder. Jump right through the dark tunnel as shown at the end of the previous chapter.
  2. Walk slowly so you do not scare off the large fly.
  3. Wait when you reach the debris in front of it.
  4. Run and jump to grab one of the fly's legs.
  5. Let it carry you up to the ledge above the ladder.
  6. If you miss its leg, go back left until it settles. Jump again.
