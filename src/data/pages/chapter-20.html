Chapter 20

Beams and guns

  1. Go right and jump the gap.
  2. Two sets of beams are ahead, one vertical and one horizontal. If you touch them, machine guns fire at you.
  3. Jump right so you touch the vertical beam in the air.
  4. Quickly jump back left to the platform as both guns fire.

Top gun and bottom gun

  1. This gets the top gun moving. Go left and wait on top of the left gun.
  2. Trigger the top gun here. Run out of the way so it shoots the bottom gun.
  3. Do this once more. The bottom gun is destroyed.
  4. Jump right past the other gun.
  5. Keep right. Walk down the hill to a pair of Boxes and the next chapter.
